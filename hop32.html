HOP32
HOP32 is a massively local multiplayer platformer built around a deliberately simple interface: thirty-two players, thirty-two buttons, and one shared journey. Players hop together across a shifting landscape, turning timing, noise, bodies, and collective attention into the game’s central mechanics.
The project was created during a two-week game jam by a San Jose local game-dev trio and has since been played as a public arcade-style installation at local events.

Public play

HOP32 has appeared in San Jose through Guildhouse, the SubZERO Arts Festival, and the San José Museum of Art’s Juneteenth Game Zone. It is designed to be encountered together: a game that turns a room full of people into a temporary controller.
