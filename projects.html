Arcade Cabinets

I build DIY arcade cabinets which are populated with games from our local Game Developers Club.

The arcade cabinets have been exhibited at Zero1, Rockage, Maker Faire, Sub Zero, and numerous shows at SJSU.
Arcade Page

Interventions in Game Spaces

I work in Virtual Environments as part of my social practice and as part of a collective. Our projects include creating NGO aid organizations and opposing the war in World of Warcraft, operating a gallery and New Media Center in Second Life, hosting artist in residencies in online environments.

Video

Teaching / Research
